# Chapter I — Becoming a 10x Founder
*The 10x Method*, pages 18-25 (Foreword starts p.15)

## What this chapter covers

The mindset chapter — 10 principles Hexa has observed across its 50+ launched companies, that separate founders who build enduring companies from those who don't. Less "how-to", more "how to think."

## The 10 principles (with the one-line essence of each)

1. **Have a long-term vision, and the first step to get there** — the "domino effect": today's action is the first domino toward the long-term vision.
2. **Balance conviction with adaptability** — be ultra-bullish on the vision, ultra-flexible on the first, second, third attempt to get there. Treat early moves as hypotheses to test, not positions to defend.
3. **Execute relentlessly** — founders stay hands-on; if a customer flags a deal-breaking gap, ship a fix that same evening.
4. **Create velocity with routines** — Hexa's own operating rhythm across 50+ companies: quarterly goals (zoom out, set OKRs) + weekly sprints (zoom in, track metrics).
5. **Keep your ego out of it** — low-ego founders hire people smarter than themselves and pivot instead of clinging to an idea to protect their identity.
6. **Build together** — no founder builds alone; the team is built by founders, but can be undone by founders' egos alone.
7. **Think global from day one** — 10x founders speak English internally, hire globally even when small, design for cross-border scalability, and seek international design partners — global *intent*, not necessarily global footprint, on day 1.
8. **Be a missionary, not a mercenary** — chasing impact and long-term value creation, not just an exit. Investors, teams, and users can feel the difference.
9. **Build resilience through purpose** — resilience comes from a felt "why," not discipline alone.
10. **Lead with kindness** — kindness builds trust; people (employees, investors, fellow founders) go the extra mile for kind founders.
