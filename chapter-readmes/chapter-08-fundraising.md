# Chapter VIII — Fundraising
*The 10x Method*, pages 202-229

## What this chapter covers

When to raise, building your story from day 1, structuring your round, choosing the right investors, how much to raise, running a tight process, and post-raise investor communication. Closes with an interview with Evan Testa, Cofounder of Roundtable (p.226).

## Key sequencing rule (p.208-209)

Front-load all prep (story, exec summary, deck, data room, lawyers) before the roadshow starts. **Nail the narrative before touching slides** — get the executive summary to ~95% clarity first; working in slides too early leads to weak framing.

## Key framework: the executive summary, 7 questions (p.209-210)

What opportunity → Why you → Step one → Path to $100M ARR → Why now → Why you'll win → Key takeaway.

## Key framework: the pitch deck, 11 sections (p.210-211)

Vision & ambition → Pain/opportunity → Proposed solution → Founder-market fit → Market size & landscape → Product overview → Pricing & business model → Traction & proof points → Team → Strategic roadmap → Path to $100M+ ARR. Plus 6 expert tips from Amaury Sepulchre (no exit slides, TAM discipline, include a "Reasons not to invest" slide, etc.)

## Key framework: the data room, 5 sections (p.212)

Executive Summary → Market → Product → GTM → Team — centralized in a single shareable Notion doc.

## Other notable sections

- **Anticipating objections** (p.209): prepare data-backed responses to predictable investor pushback in advance.
- **Structuring your round** (p.213-215): instrument choice, cap table hygiene, choosing counsel.
- **Choosing the right investors** (p.216): who's around the table, benefits of angels, partnering with people not just capital.
- **How much to raise** (p.217-218): raising more isn't always better; you don't choose your valuation.
- **Running a tight process** (p.220-223): don't talk to VCs too early; create momentum at go-time; negotiate with strategy not emotion.
- **Post-raise** (p.224-225): make investor updates consistent; think about the next round from day one.

## Notable quotes

> "Preparation is everything. Rehearse your deck, refine the story, test your numbers. And before you pitch your 'dream' VCs, start with 4-5 funds that you know aren't the right fit. Use them as live practice." — **Olivier Pailhes, Cofounder of Aircall**

**From Amaury Sepulchre, Partner and Cofounder of Hexa** (p.211): "No exit slides... If an investor asks for it, they're not aligned with your long-term ambition." / "TAM discipline: avoid vague or inflated TAMs... A clear go-to-market beats a trillion-dollar pie chart."
