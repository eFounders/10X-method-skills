# Chapter IX — ESG for Early Stage
*The 10x Method*, pages 230-235

(Note: this chapter is labeled "Chapter IV" in the book's own table of contents by a layout typo — it is sequentially the 9th chapter, following Fundraising and preceding the Conclusion.)

## What this chapter covers

Why ESG matters even pre-Series A, and a practical, non-overwhelming checklist for founders who aren't yet measuring carbon footprint or running formal board routines.

## Key framing (p.232)

Even if a business doesn't directly tackle ESG issues, *how* it's built inevitably does. Starting early is efficient — small teams and nimble operations mean ESG practices require little effort but create outsized long-term value. Practical drivers: vendors/partners increasingly hold ESG standards, investors factor it into decisions, top talent seeks responsible employers.

## Key framework: the E/S/G checklist (p.233-235, verbatim)

- **Environment**: low-impact operations (remote-first, minimal printing/swag), carbon footprint awareness (not full audits yet), sustainable procurement (ethical vendors, energy-efficient cloud).
- **Social**: DEI (concise bias-checked JDs, standardized scorecards, diverse candidate pools, salary grids, ESOP), employee well-being (safe environment, flexibility, regular feedback, shared mission).
- **Governance**: founding team alignment as early governance (regular check-ins, documented decisions, tie-breaker rules), preparing for future governance (board diversity/independence), data ethics (AI/data guidelines, early SOC2/GDPR awareness, strong data protection).
