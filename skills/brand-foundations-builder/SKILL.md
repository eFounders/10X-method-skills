# Brand Foundations Builder

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter VII — "Brand & Communications", pages 183-184.

Full template with worked examples (Notion, Stripe, Figma, Superhuman, Slack, Linear): see `references/brand-foundations-template.md` — read it before running this, and use those examples as calibration for tone and specificity.

## What this skill does

Forces clarity on a single, one-page brand foundation document by answering the book's 6 core questions. This isn't a 50-page brand guideline — it's a "just good enough" foundation that keeps every piece of company communication consistent.

## The 6 questions

1. What's your driving force? (the "why" beyond product or profit)
2. What's your long-term vision? (5-10 year view of the world you're creating)
3. What's your mission? (one-liner: what the product does today, for whom)
4. Who do you serve? (specific segment, needs, problems, aspirations)
5. What is your core benefit? (the main value delivered)
6. What else makes you special? (the unique qualities/approach that differentiate you)

## Workflow

1. **Interview the founder** on each of the 6 questions. If they already have rough answers (from a pitch deck, website, or conversation), extract and refine those rather than starting from scratch.
2. **Push for specificity** on each answer — the book's examples (Notion, Stripe, Figma) are all sharp and concrete, not generic ("we help businesses grow" is not a real answer to any of these). If an answer is vague, ask a follow-up question to sharpen it.
3. **Check for internal consistency**: does the "who you serve" answer match the "core benefit" answer? Does the mission actually serve the long-term vision, or does it feel disconnected?
4. **Fill the table** using the exact 6-row structure from the reference file.
5. **Offer the natural next step**: once foundations are set, pair with the `brand-voice-finder` skill to turn this into a "brand feeling" and voice adjectives for actual copywriting.

## Output format

A markdown table with 2 columns (Question | Answer), one row per question, written in the founder's own specific language — not filled with placeholders or generic startup-speak.
