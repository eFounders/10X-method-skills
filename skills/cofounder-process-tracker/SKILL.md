# Cofounder Process Tracker

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter III — "Choosing Your Co-founder", page 64. Section: "Don't rush the process."

## Why this matters (per the book)

"You need enough signal to feel confident this is someone you can build a company with. One great conversation isn't enough." The book is explicit that this is a high-stakes decision that deserves real time across different contexts, not a single good meeting.

## The 4 safeguards (verbatim from the book)

1. **Have at least 3 conversations** before making any decision — ideally including one informal one (a walk, a coffee, a dinner). "Three is the bare minimum — ideally, you'd have more." The point is to drop the pitch and just talk like people.
2. **Work together for a week** — a short POC (mapping out the product, exploring directions, talking to a few design partners). The output matters less than the process: how ideas evolve, how you handle ambiguity, whether it feels like progress. "A week is usually enough to get a meaningful sense of the dynamic. If you're still unsure after two, it's probably worth pausing to reflect on why."
3. **Get a second opinion** from someone you trust in the same domain as the candidate — a friendly CTO to review a technical profile's GitHub or have a quick call, a CEO/business lead to skim notes or take a short call for a business profile.
4. **Run references** — speak to at least 2 former managers and 2 former reports, and offer to do the same for them. Never skip this step.

## Workflow

1. **Ask the founder where they are in the process** with this specific candidate: how many real conversations so far (and what kind — formal pitch vs. informal), whether they've worked together on anything yet, whether a second opinion or references have happened.
2. **Build a status tracker** against the 4 safeguards: ✅ Done / 🔲 Not yet / ⏳ In progress.
3. **If they're about to commit with safeguards missing**, say so directly — this is exactly the scenario the book warns against ("don't rush the process"). Don't soften it because the founder seems excited.
4. **Suggest concrete next steps** to close the gaps — e.g. if no informal conversation has happened yet, suggest a specific low-key setting (a walk, a meal) before any final conversation about commitment.

## Output format

A 4-row checklist (safeguard | status | what's missing/next step), followed by a direct one-line verdict: ready to decide, or not yet — and why.
