# Early-Stage ESG Checklist

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter IX — "ESG for Early Stage", pages 232-235.

Full checklist verbatim, organized by E/S/G: see `references/esg-checklist-full.md` — read it before generating a personalized checklist, and use its exact items as the base list.

## Why this matters early (per the book)

Even if a business doesn't directly tackle ESG issues, *how* it's built inevitably does — from who you employ and how you lead, to emissions generated and governance structures put in place. Starting at the early stage is efficient: with a small team and nimble operations, embedding ESG practices requires relatively little effort but creates outsized long-term value. Practical reasons it matters: vendors/partners increasingly hold ESG standards, investors factor ESG into decisions, and top talent seeks responsible employers.

This is explicitly **not** about carbon audits, culture celebrations, or formal board routines yet — those come later (Series A+). This is about laying groundwork now.

## Workflow

1. **Get the founder's current stage and practices**: team size, remote/hybrid/in-office, hiring practices so far, whether they handle AI/large datasets, whether cofounder governance is already documented.
2. **Walk through each of the 3 pillars** (Environment / Social / Governance) using the full item list from the reference file, and mark each item as:
   - ✅ Already in place
   - 🔲 Not yet, but easy to add now
   - ⏳ Not relevant yet at this stage
3. **Prioritize the 🔲 items** — don't just dump the full checklist; identify the 3-5 highest-leverage items to tackle first given the founder's specific situation (e.g. a founder about to hire their first employees should prioritize the DEI/scorecard items; a founder building with AI/data should prioritize the data ethics items).
4. **Keep the tone proportionate to stage** — reinforce explicitly that this isn't about doing everything at once; awareness and small deliberate steps are the goal, not perfection.

## Output format

A checklist grouped by E / S / G, each item marked ✅ / 🔲 / ⏳ based on the founder's answers, followed by a short "Start here" list of the 3-5 highest-priority next actions.
