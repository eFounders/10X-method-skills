# Founder Branding Content Planner

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter VII — "Brand & Communications", pages 193-196. Includes an extended insight from Thibaud Elziere, Partner and Cofounder at Hexa.

## Why this matters (p.193)

While the company is still unknown, the founder is immediately visible and accessible — personal brand is often the most powerful early marketing channel, the approachable entry point that draws people in before they understand the product.

## Align with brand feeling, but stay authentic (p.193-194)

Personal brand should exist in the same universe as the company's brand, but doesn't need to be identical. If the company's feeling is "empowering, confident," the founder doesn't need to become a caricature of empowerment personally. The most effective approach: find the natural overlap between the authentic self and the company's values — personal content can be more casual, opinionated, or playful than the company voice, and that's fine.

## Tell your story (p.194, verbatim method)

The personal story (why the founder started the company, what problem they experienced) is one of the most valuable brand assets — it humanizes the company in ways product talk can't. To tap into it:
- **Identify the emotional core**: what feeling drove the decision to start — frustration, inspiration, determination? Connect it to the desired brand feeling.
- **Find the relatable moments**: which parts of the journey will resonate with the audience's own experience?
- **Connect past to present**: show how personal history directly shaped the company's approach or values.
- **Keep it authentic**: share genuine challenges alongside victories — perfect stories are less believable and relatable.

Two parallel but connected brands (personal + company) strengthen each other when managed deliberately, creating more entry points for discovery.

## Thibaud Elziere's principles (p.195-196, Partner & Cofounder at Hexa)

- **Authentic alignment**: find a format/channel that matches who you actually are, not a copied playbook — Paul Graham chose long-form essays, others thrive on X, podcasts, or stage talks. It took Thibaud time to find LinkedIn with a casual, authentic tone — which also became a powerful hiring tool.
- **Consistency over time**: results come from consistency, not intensity. Building an audience takes years even now — cadence matters more than format (daily Instagram story, 1-2 LinkedIn posts/week, weekly podcast, quarterly blog post — any of these work if genuinely sustained). Consistency doesn't mean rigidity — tone, themes, and positioning evolve as the founder does.
- **Diversify thoughtfully**: focus on one primary channel first (compounding effect), but stay open to external opportunities (journalist interviews, podcast guest spots, conference talks) that bring new audiences back to the main channel — be selective, since the personal brand is at stake.
- **It's a dialogue, not a monologue**: use every platform to listen, engage, and build a participative community, not just broadcast.

## Workflow

1. **Pull in brand feeling/voice** if defined (`brand-voice-finder`) — founder content should feel like the same person as the brand.
2. **Mine the founder's real story** using the 4-step method above (emotional core → relatable moments → past-to-present connection → authentic imperfection).
3. **Help pick one primary channel** matched to the founder's actual personality and strengths (long-form writer? conversational? camera-comfortable?) rather than defaulting to "LinkedIn because everyone does LinkedIn."
4. **Set a realistic, sustainable cadence** — push back on ambitious plans (daily posting) that won't survive contact with a busy founder's schedule; a lower, consistent cadence beats sporadic bursts.
5. **Build a simple content bucket list** (not a rigid calendar): building-in-public updates, lessons from hard decisions, hiring/culture moments, customer stories (with consent), personal reflections tied to the mission.
6. **Push back on generic "thought leadership."** If a draft reads like something anyone could have written, flag it and dig for the specific personal detail that makes it theirs.

## Output format

1. The founder's story, structured through the 4-step method (as a short narrative, not just bullet answers)
2. Recommended primary channel + rationale
3. A sustainable cadence recommendation
4. 4-6 content buckets + 3-5 concrete post ideas tied to real details the founder shared
