# GTM Ritual Setup

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter VI — "Acquiring the Very First Customers", pages 162-163.

## Framing (per the book's operating philosophy)

This mirrors the same rhythm-over-planning approach used for product (see `product-rituals-setup`) and for the company overall (Chapter I's "create velocity with routines" — quarterly goals + weekly sprints). Applied to GTM specifically: **quarterly GTM rituals** to zoom out and reset direction, and **weekly GTM sprints** to maintain momentum and track what's actually moving.

## Workflow

1. **Get the founder's current GTM state**: what stage they're at (pre-DP, running a DP program, scaling from 10 to 100 clients — see `design-partner-deck-builder`, `bant-qualifier`, `icp-definer-refiner` for those stages), and what they're currently tracking, if anything.
2. **Design the quarterly ritual**: a session to zoom out on GTM — review what worked/didn't over the past quarter (channels, messaging, conversion), reset ICP if needed (pair with `icp-definer-refiner`), and set 2-3 concrete GTM objectives for the coming quarter (this should plug into the same milestone roadmap format as product — see `milestone-roadmap-builder`).
3. **Design the weekly sprint**: a short, consistent weekly check-in — pipeline status (how many leads at each BANT stage, see `bant-qualifier`), what moved, what's blocked, and the single most important GTM action for the coming week.
4. **Keep it lightweight** — per the book's broader philosophy, the value is in the consistency of a simple ritual, not the sophistication of the tracking system; a founder tracking GTM in a simple doc or spreadsheet consistently beats an elaborate CRM used sporadically.
5. **Tie it back to founder-led GTM** — the book is explicit that GTM stays founder-led well past the first 10 clients; these rituals are for the founder to run personally, not to delegate away early.

## Output format

Two ready-to-use templates:
- **Quarterly GTM review**: agenda with 3-4 sections (channel/messaging review, ICP check, next quarter's 2-3 objectives)
- **Weekly GTM sprint**: a short recurring check-in template (pipeline snapshot, what moved, top priority for the week)
