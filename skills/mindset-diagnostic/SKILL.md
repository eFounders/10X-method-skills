# 10x Founder Mindset Diagnostic

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter I — "Becoming a 10x Founder", pages 21-25.

## What this skill does

Walks a founder through Hexa's 10 mindset principles (observed across 50+ launched companies) and produces an honest, specific diagnosis of where they're strong and where they're drifting — not a generic pep talk.

## The 10 principles

1. Have a long-term vision, and the first step to get there (the "domino effect")
2. Balance conviction with adaptability (bullish on vision, flexible on execution)
3. Execute relentlessly (stay hands-on, ship fast)
4. Create velocity with routines (quarterly goals + weekly sprints)
5. Keep your ego out of it (hire people smarter than you, admit when wrong)
6. Build together (a company is built by the team, undone by founders' egos alone)
7. Think global from day one (global intent, even if small)
8. Be a missionary, not a mercenary (long-term impact over quick exit)
9. Build resilience through purpose (a felt "why", not just discipline)
10. Lead with kindness (builds trust that compounds)

## Workflow

1. **Ask the founder to describe their current situation honestly** — what's working, what's hard right now, any specific tension or decision they're facing.
2. **Score each principle** as a rough gut-check based on what they've shared: 🟢 Strong / 🟡 Inconsistent / 🔴 Struggling — don't force a score if there's no evidence either way, mark it "not enough signal" instead of guessing.
3. **Go deep on the 2-3 principles that seem most relevant** to whatever they're currently facing (e.g. if they mention a cofounder conflict, dig into #5 ego and #6 build together; if they're stuck in planning, dig into #2 conviction/adaptability and #4 routines).
4. **Be direct, not therapeutic** — this isn't a wellbeing check-in, it's an operating diagnosis. If ego or lack of routine seems to be the real issue, say so plainly.
5. **End with one concrete next action**, not ten — the book's own philosophy is momentum over exhaustive planning, so the diagnosis should practice what it preaches.

## Output format

A short scorecard (10 rows: principle | status | one-line evidence), followed by a focused 3-paragraph diagnosis on the most relevant 2-3 principles, and one concrete next action.

## Caution

This is a mindset/operating diagnostic, not a mental health assessment. If a founder's message suggests genuine burnout or distress beyond normal startup stress, respond with care and suggest they talk to someone they trust — don't just push through with the framework.
