# Objection Anticipation Prep

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter VIII — "Fundraising", page 209.

## Core guidance (per the book)

Anticipating key investor objections is essential — things like "I'm not investing because a major competitor just raised a big round" or "the market looks too crowded" are common and predictable. Founders need clear, sharp responses ready in advance. For each objection, build a well-structured argument ideally supported by precise, data-backed reasoning: market segmentation, differentiation, go-to-market insights, or unique traction numbers.

Some answers belong in the data room (e.g. competitive landscape, pipeline data — see `data-room-builder`); others should be rehearsed and delivered convincingly live in the room. The best founders dry-run these hard questions in advance, like a stress test, so nothing catches them off guard.

## Workflow

1. **Get the founder's current narrative** (ideally their executive summary — see `executive-summary-builder`) and any specific concerns they're worried investors will raise.
2. **Generate the predictable objection list** — beyond what the founder already fears, add the classic categories the book flags: competitive funding news, crowded market perception, team gaps, unproven business model, small market size, timing skepticism ("why hasn't this been done already").
3. **For each objection, build a data-backed response** — push for specifics (a number, a segmentation detail, a differentiation point) rather than a purely narrative rebuttal. If the founder doesn't have the data yet, flag it as a gap to fill before the roadshow rather than papering over it with a vague answer.
4. **Sort each response**: "belongs in the data room" (detailed backup material) vs. "must be rehearsed live" (a sharp, memorized verbal answer for the room).
5. **Suggest a dry-run**: recommend the founder rehearse the hardest 2-3 objections out loud, ideally with someone playing a skeptical investor — per the book's own advice to practice on funds that aren't the "dream" targets first.

## Output format

A table: **Objection | Data-backed response | Where it lives (data room / rehearsed live)** — covering both the founder's stated worries and the standard predictable objections from the book's list.
