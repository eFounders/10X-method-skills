# Outbound Sequence Builder

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter VI — "Acquiring the Very First Customers", pages 152-160. Section: "Not-so-cold outbound," within "From 10 to 100 clients."

## Framing (per the book's chapter context)

The book positions this stage explicitly as still **founder-led** — GTM doesn't get handed off to a sales team yet. Outbound at this stage isn't traditional cold outreach; it leverages what's already been built: existing design partners (via a lookalike strategy — targeting prospects who resemble the DPs already succeeding with the product), personal credibility, and specific, personalized messaging tied to a well-defined ICP (see `icp-definer-refiner`).

## Workflow

1. **Get the ICP and any existing design partner success stories** — outbound should reference real, specific proof points (a DP's result, a concrete use case) rather than generic value propositions.
2. **Use a lookalike approach**: identify prospects who resemble the founder's best-performing design partners or customers (same industry, same role, same pain point profile) rather than broad, generic targeting.
3. **Keep messaging personal and founder-voiced** — this is not a sales-team template; it should read like a founder reaching out directly, referencing something specific about the prospect (not a mail-merge field).
4. **Draft a short sequence** (2-3 touches max at this stage — this isn't a 7-step drip campaign): an initial personalized outreach referencing a specific, relevant proof point; a brief follow-up if no response; and a graceful close-the-loop message if there's still no response.
5. **Tie into the qualification framework** — once a prospect responds with interest, hand off to `bant-qualifier` to qualify the call, and to `design-partner-deck-builder` if the conversation is now a formal DP pitch.

## A note on positioning

This particular skill's territory overlaps with dedicated cold-outreach/sequencing tools — its distinct value here is staying explicitly founder-led, personal, and tied to the 10x Method's specific GTM sequence (lookalike targeting from real DPs, not generic list-building), rather than being a general-purpose sequencing tool.

## Output format

1. The lookalike targeting logic (who to reach, and why, based on existing DP/customer proof points)
2. A 2-3 touch outbound sequence, each message short, personal, and founder-voiced
3. A note on what happens next once someone responds (hand-off to BANT qualification)
