# Pitch Deck Structure Builder

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter VIII — "Fundraising", pages 210-211.

Full section-by-section template and the 6 expert tips from Amaury Sepulchre (Hexa): see `references/pitch-deck-template.md` — read it before building or reviewing a deck.

## Precondition

The book is explicit: don't build the deck before the narrative is nailed. If the founder hasn't already gone through `executive-summary-builder`, suggest doing that first — the deck should be a visual expansion of an already-clear story, not where the story gets figured out.

## The 11 sections

1. Vision & ambition
2. Pain / opportunity
3. Proposed solution / offer
4. Founder–market fit
5. Market size & landscape
6. Product overview
7. Pricing & business model (if relevant)
8. Traction & proof points
9. Team
10. Strategic roadmap & fundraising rationale
11. Path to $100M+ ARR

## The 6 expert tips to apply (verbatim rules)

1. Tailor the story — this structure isn't exhaustive; cut anything that doesn't serve the narrative.
2. Hypothesis-driven funding — state what this round is meant to prove or disprove, and the metrics used to track it.
3. No exit slides at this stage.
4. TAM discipline — be specific about entry point and expansion, not a vague trillion-dollar numberz.
5. Include a "Reasons not to invest" slide.
6. Nail the path to $100M+ ARR — the hardest slide, and the most important.

## Workflow

**If reviewing an existing deck:**
1. Map each existing slide against the 11-section structure — flag missing sections and sections that are present but weak.
2. Check against each of the 6 tips explicitly (does it have an exit slide that shouldn't be there? Is the TAM vague/inflated? Is there a "reasons not to invest" slide?).
3. Give direct, section-by-section feedback — not generic "make it more compelling" notes.

**If building a new deck outline:**
1. Gather the founder's inputs (ideally reusing their executive summary output).
2. Produce a slide-by-slide outline: for each of the 11 sections, a suggested slide title and 3-5 bullet points of content specific to their story.
3. Apply the 6 tips as constraints while drafting (no exit slide, TAM discipline, etc.), and explicitly note where a "Reasons not to invest" slide should go.

## Output format

A slide-by-slide outline table: **Slide # | Section | Suggested title | Key content (bullets)** — followed by a short checklist confirming each of the 6 expert tips has been applied or explicitly addressed.
