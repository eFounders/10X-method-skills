# Product Rituals Setup

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter IV — "Building the MVP", pages 82-83.

## Why this matters (per the book)

Even with just one or two founders, establishing a clear rhythm for product discussions, prioritization, and execution keeps things moving efficiently. The key is consistency — these rituals maintain focus, help iterate faster, and ensure small incremental progress compounds over time.

## The weekly product committee (verbatim structure from the book)

- **Cadence**: every week, same time.
- **Duration**: 30 minutes to one hour — keep it fast and focused.
- **Who's involved**: everyone working on the product (even if that's just the founder alone).

**What happens:**
1. **Review what was shipped** — every feature, bug fix, or improvement from the past week should be demoed, even if it's rough.
2. **Refine priorities for the upcoming week** — what's the one most important thing to focus on next?
3. **Address blockers** — anything slowing the team down?

This pairs naturally with the milestone roadmap (see `milestone-roadmap-builder`) — the weekly committee is the mechanism that keeps that quarterly roadmap honest week to week.

## Workflow

1. **Confirm team size and current state** — solo founder, small team, or several people working on product — the ritual scales down but the structure stays the same even solo (demoing to yourself/a cofounder still works).
2. **Set the fixed cadence** — help the founder commit to a specific day/time, not "whenever we have time."
3. **Draft the recurring agenda** using the 3-part structure (review shipped → refine next priority → address blockers), tailored with placeholders for their actual current work if they share it.
4. **Emphasize the discipline over the content** — the book's point isn't that this specific format is magic, it's that *consistency* is what compounds; stress that skipping weeks defeats the purpose.

## Output format

A ready-to-use recurring meeting template: cadence/duration/participants, followed by the 3-part agenda with a few tailored prompts under each section based on what the founder shared about their current product state.
