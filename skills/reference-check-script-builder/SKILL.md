# Reference Check Script Builder

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter V — "Hiring and Retaining Early Talent", page 127. Section: "Do not skip reference checks."

## The core rule (per the book)

The book is explicit that reference checks should never be skipped, even under hiring time pressure — this mirrors the same discipline the book applies to cofounder vetting (2 former managers + 2 former reports, see `cofounder-process-tracker`), applied here to hiring a candidate. The goal is to hear from people who've actually worked with the candidate day-to-day, not just people the candidate hand-picked to make them look good — so it's worth explicitly asking the candidate for a mix, and being alert if they're reluctant to provide former managers specifically.

## Workflow

1. **Confirm who to call**: aim for at least 2 former managers and 2 former direct reports (or peers, if the role has no reports) — enough spread to see the candidate from above and below.
2. **Build the question list**, tied back to the specific scorecard criteria for this role if available (see `scorecard-builder`) rather than generic questions — e.g. if "gets things done" is a scorecard criterion, ask a reference for a specific example of that.
3. **Include a few standard calibration questions** regardless of role:
   - What were their core responsibilities, and how did their role evolve over time?
   - What would you say is their biggest strength, with a specific example?
   - What's one area they'd need to grow in for a fast-moving, high-ownership startup role?
   - Would you work with them again? Why or why not?
   - (If a manager) How did they perform under ambiguity or pressure specifically?
4. **Flag any hesitation or vague answers** as data, not noise — the book's framing throughout the hiring chapter (scorecards, structured process) is about removing gut-feeling bias; the same applies here.
5. **Offer to do the same in return** — the book notes this reciprocity (from the cofounder chapter's parallel process) builds trust and is a fair practice with senior candidates.

## Output format

1. A short target list (2 managers + 2 reports, or role-appropriate equivalent)
2. A tailored question script (5-8 questions), linked to the role's actual must-have scorecard criteria where possible
3. A one-line reminder: don't skip this step even under time pressure
