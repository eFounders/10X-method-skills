# Weekly Momentum Email Builder

Source: *The 10x Method* (Hexa), Chapter I — "Becoming a 10x Founder", pages 22-23.

## The ritual this is based on

> "The best routine I ever put in place — and stuck to for ten years — was sending an email to the entire team every Sunday evening. A clear, no-frills recap: key metrics, how they moved, and the top priorities for the week ahead. Same format, same discipline, every time. Even when we were 500 or 600 people, I never skipped it... a company should be run week by week." — Olivier Pailhes, Cofounder of Aircall

The book frames this as part of a broader "create velocity with routines" principle: quarterly goals set direction, weekly sprints maintain momentum. This email is the weekly half of that rhythm.

## Design principles (per the book)

- **Same format every time** — no reinventing the structure week to week; consistency is the point.
- **No-frills** — a clear recap, not a narrative essay.
- **Sent on a fixed cadence** — same day, same time (Sunday evening in the book's example), without exception, even during hard weeks.
- **Transparent even when the news is bad** — "if we missed our goals, we didn't wait around."

## Workflow

1. **Get the founder's current metrics and priorities**: what are the 2-4 key metrics that matter right now, how did they move this week, and what are the top 1-3 priorities for next week.
2. **Fill a fixed template** (see below) — resist the temptation to add extra sections; the discipline is in the sameness.
3. **If the founder doesn't have clear weekly metrics yet**, help them pick 2-4 before building the email — this ritual only works if there's something concrete to report on.
4. **Suggest the cadence commitment** explicitly: same day, same time, every week, no skipping — even (especially) on rough weeks.

## Template

```
Subject: Week of [date] — Recap

Key metrics this week:
- [Metric 1]: [value] ([↑/↓/→] vs last week)
- [Metric 2]: [value] ([↑/↓/→] vs last week)

What happened:
[2-3 sentences, no-frills, including bad news if any]

Top priorities for next week:
1. [Priority 1]
2. [Priority 2]
3. [Priority 3]
```

## Output format

The filled-in email template above, ready to send, plus a one-line reminder about the cadence discipline that makes this ritual work (same format, same time, no skipping).
